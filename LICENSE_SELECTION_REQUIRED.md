# Licence selection required

No public reuse licence has been selected by the authors. Do not publish this repository until the authors have
approved appropriate licences for both:

1. software/source code; and
2. derived metadata, manifests, result tables and figures.

Licence selection is a legal and collective-author decision. This package therefore does not infer or insert an
MIT, Apache, GPL, Creative Commons or other licence. In the absence of a licence, copyright is retained and reuse
permission is not granted by default.

Raw private seed images and copyrighted benchmark media remain excluded regardless of the software licence unless
their separate redistribution rights are documented.
