# Licence selection required

No repository-wide public reuse licence has been selected by the authors. Although the repository is currently
publicly visible, the authors must approve appropriate licences before the DOI-bearing release for both:

1. software/source code; and
2. derived metadata, manifests, result tables and figures.

Licence selection is a legal and collective-author decision. This package therefore does not infer or insert an
MIT, Apache, GPL, Creative Commons or other repository-wide licence. In the absence of a licence, copyright is
retained and reuse permission is not granted by default. `contracts/ImageRegistryV2.sol` is the sole current
file-level exception: its SPDX header explicitly licenses that file under MIT.

Raw private seed images and copyrighted benchmark media remain excluded regardless of the software licence unless
their separate redistribution rights are documented.
